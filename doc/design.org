*** Dungeon Run
**** Concept
- This is the core game loop.
- Tetris gameplay, fighting enemies.
- Lines cleared cause damage by line cleared.
- Lines cleared also add distance from left edge by lines cleared.
- Different Tetronomes mean different materials
- Different Enemies also mean different materials
- Different areas increase the difficulty of enemies and speed.

***** Looting
- Dungeon runs are infinite until you die.
- Periodic boss enemies with garunteed loot drops.
- Enemies get stronger and you start retreating faster as you play.
- As you get farther, there is a greater chance for enemies to drop special weapons or equipment or crafting materials
- As you get progress through higher tiers of each area, loot chances are higher as well (but enemies harder) 
- Different dungeon areas may have different crafting materials / loot types as well (still thinking about this)

***** Damage Types
- Enemies do various damage types that can be futher mitigated with resistance bonuses
